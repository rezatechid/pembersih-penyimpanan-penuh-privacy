1. About the app

Storage Cleaner & File Tools (Indonesian name: Pembersih Penyimpanan Penuh; package ID com.pembersihpenyimpananpenuh.app) is developed by reza tech. The app helps users inspect device storage, review detected files and folders, and perform cleanup actions supported by Android and authorized by the user.

Core file scanning and cleanup operations take place locally on the device. The developer does not operate a server for uploading users' personal files. However, the app uses third-party advertising and consent services, as described below.

2. Data and access used

  • Files and storage metadata: with appropriate permissions, the app may access names, types, sizes, locations, timestamps, and previews of accessible files for scanning, duplicate detection, storage analysis, file management, and review before cleanup. Photo, video, or document contents may be opened locally when the user requests a preview.
  • App information: the app list uses information available through Android APIs and package-visibility restrictions. The app does not request Usage Access or read app-usage statistics through that permission. It does not assume another app's private cache can be deleted automatically.
  • Local preferences and history: display or language settings, exclusions, feature choices, and cleanup-history records may be stored on the device to support app functionality.
  • Advertising data: Google Mobile Ads SDK/AdMob and Google's consent tools may process device and interaction information as described in section 4.

To identify identical duplicates, the app may stream accessible candidate contents to calculate local SHA-256 hashes, including through MediaStore URIs and authorized SAF folders. Matching names or sizes alone do not prove duplication. Scan and hashing limits vary by source and mode; incomplete results are identified as such.

The app may read photo/video content locally to load thumbnails in result lists or show a preview requested by the user. Thumbnails use bounded dimensions; access or decoding failures may produce a placeholder. The app implementation does not upload file contents, URIs, hashes, or inspection findings to a developer server or include them in advertising requests.

When users select a file or folder through the Storage Access Framework, the app may retain URI permission when supported by the provider and granted by Android. Selected location identifiers may be stored locally for subsequent access. The app checks retained grants; revoked permissions or unavailable locations are not treated as accessible. This does not open other apps' private directories. Cloud providers may require internet access and apply their own policies.

The APK scanner reads a user-selected APK up to 2 GiB, subject to device capability and resource limits. SHA-256, metadata, permissions, components, integrity, and signatures are inspected offline. Partial checks are identified as incomplete; results do not guarantee an APK is free of malware. APKs are not installed or executed. A private temporary copy is removed on a best-effort basis after completion or cancellation; cleanup failure or process termination can leave a copy in app cache.

3. How data is used

Local data supports scan results, candidate classification, previews, user selections and exclusions, confirmation of requested actions, and accurate reporting of completed operations. Files marked Review required or Protected are not automatically eligible for deletion. The app does not claim it can clean all system caches or other apps' private data.

Selecting a candidate for review does not authorize deletion. Shared-file deletion uses supported access/provider mechanisms after applicable identity, access, eligibility, exclusion, and confirmation checks. Android system approval is requested where required. Keepers, protected items, and candidates that do not meet deletion requirements must not be processed as eligible. Opening Gallery does not establish that Gallery trash was deleted. Unverified outcomes are not counted as successful deletion or freed space. Personal files are not deleted automatically.

Cleanup of this app's own cache is limited to disposable files meeting the app's policy and validation checks. It does not grant access to another app's private cache, close other apps, or prove increased RAM or device performance.

4. Advertising, Google AdMob, and consent

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent information and choices where required. When advertising services operate, Google may collect or receive IP addresses (which can be used to estimate general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, and other applicable device/app identifiers. These may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable settings and consent choices.

Third-party practices can vary by region, SDK version, and privacy choices. The app does not send the contents of users' photos, videos, documents, or personal file lists to the developer for advertising. Google's processing is governed by its policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Users may manage available ad/privacy choices through the app's consent flow and device settings.

Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 has its own collection and sharing practices, including IP addresses, product interactions, diagnostics, and applicable identifiers. UMP 4.0.0 manages privacy information and choices and presents forms when required; it does not guarantee that all advertising data processing stops. Ad requests follow the app's consent checks. Available choices and forms can vary by region and configuration. Refusing consent does not block core features; changing a choice does not automatically mean Google processes no data. According to Google documentation, user data collected by Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS.

5. Android permissions

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), when requested and granted on supported Android versions: helps scan and manage files in lawfully accessible shared storage. This permission does not grant unrestricted access to every other app's private data.
  • Media/storage access and/or document picker: lets users select or view items according to the Android version and granted permissions, including via the Storage Access Framework.
  • Usage Access: not requested or used by the app. This flow has no Accessibility service, root, or permission to force-stop other apps.
  • Internet and network state: supports ads, consent requests, and opening this privacy policy in a browser; core file scanning does not require an internet connection.

Users can deny or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Certain features may become limited, and incomplete scans should not be represented as complete.

6. Data sharing and external services

The developer does not operate a server to receive or store scanned personal files. Data processed by the advertising SDK may be transferred to Google as explained in section 4. If users open documents through third-party storage providers or apps, those providers' own permissions and policies may apply. This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; visits to the website may be processed by the hosting provider, including technical logs and IP addresses.

7. Retention and deletion

Scan metadata may be used during the app session, while preferences, exclusions, and cleaning history may be stored locally until removed using available controls, Android's Clear storage action, or app uninstallation (subject to device/system backup behavior). User-file deletion occurs only through supported, authorized, and confirmed operations; some items cannot be deleted due to Android restrictions or safety protections. Data retained by Google for advertising is subject to Google's retention policies rather than direct control by this app.

File lists, selections, and some scan/inspection results remain in session memory and can be lost when the process ends. Preferences, exclusions, retained access-location identifiers, and cleaning-history summaries may be stored locally. History includes time, category/source, counts, status, and verified sizes; the current repository retains up to 100 entries. Closing the app does not remove all this information. History can be cleared using available app controls; grants are managed through Android/provider facilities. Clearing app data does not guarantee deletion of data already handled by Google.

8. Security

File access uses available Android permissions and APIs. The app separates candidates that may be eligible for deletion from items that have not been verified. We aim to limit processing and access to what features require, but cannot guarantee absolute security for any device or third-party service.

9. User choices and rights

Users can manage storage/document access and available advertising choices through the app or Android Settings. To remove local data, use available controls or Android's Clear storage action. Contact us below for privacy questions or data managed by the developer. Advertising data controlled by Google follows Google's controls and policies.
